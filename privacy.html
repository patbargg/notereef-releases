Privacy Policy

Notereef is an app for notes, plans and personal finances, for Windows, for Android and in a browser. This page says what the app keeps about you, where, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Patryk Barszczewski, the developer of Notereef. Questions and requests: barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com.

What Notereef keeps

What Details
Your account Your email address; your password, kept only as a hash that nobody can read back; the first and last name you gave; your language, currency and first day of the week; how you arranged Home; a profile picture, if you add one.
What you put in the app Workspaces and pages with their text and the pictures and files you add to them; calendar events; tasks; finance entries (income and expenses, stocks and other assets, savings goals); shared expenses (groups, the names of the people in them, amounts); whiteboards and what is drawn on them; spreadsheets and what is in their cells; organizations and the messages written in their chat, with the pictures and files sent in them and the reactions to them, the people you blocked in the chat, and the reports you send about someone or a message there (who and why, and what the message said, kept for whoever looks after Notereef even after the message is deleted); files and folders in Drive, and the files of apps kept with your account.
Sharing Who you share a workspace, a whiteboard, a spreadsheet, an expense group or an organization with, their role, and invitations waiting for an answer.
Activity How long the app was open on each day (a number of seconds per day), for the activity chart on Home. Not what you did in it. And, so that a chat message is told on one of your devices and not on two: when the computer the app runs on was last used, and that the app is in front on your phone. Each is a single moment that the next one replaces.
Technical logs The servers the app talks to record the IP address, the time and the kind of each request for a short time, as every web server does.

Notereef has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not read your location, your contacts or other apps. It does not connect to your bank or broker: the finance entries are the ones you type in or import yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Screenshots you import (a bank's, a calendar's, a broker's) are read on your device. The pictures are not sent anywhere; only the entries you confirm are saved to your account.
  • Reminders on Android are scheduled on the phone itself, from what the app has loaded. Nothing is sent to the phone from a server.
  • The app keeps you signed in and remembers a few choices (the last language, reminder settings) in its own storage on the device.
  • The API keys you give ReefAI (see below) are kept with your account in Notereef's database, encrypted, so that they are there on every device you log in on. The app uses them only to talk to the company each of them is for. They go when you remove them in ReefAI's settings or delete the account.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To give you the app: to keep your things, show them on your devices and share them with the people you choose. This is the performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working (the technical logs). This is a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your data is not sold and not used for advertising or profiling, and no decisions about you are made automatically.

  • You.

  • A profile picture is kept at a web address that is long, random and listed nowhere, but not protected by a password.
  • A picture or file on a page, in a chat or in Drive is fetched through a link that works for a day and is given only to those who can see it.

Prices of stocks and funds in Finance come from Yahoo Finance through Notereef's own server: only the symbol of the stock or fund is sent, nothing about you. If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download under its own privacy policy.

ReefAI, the assistant in the app, runs on your computer, in the Windows app: it is Notereef's own model, which you install in ReefAI's section (from Hugging Face and GitHub, see above). It needs no key and no account with anyone, and what you write to it, the pictures you send it and what it reads to answer you are worked on on your computer and go to no other company. Your conversations with ReefAI (what you write, its answers and what it read for them) are kept with your account since 27 October 2026, so that they are the same on all your devices; pictures you send stay on the device you sent them from. You can open a conversation again, rename it or delete it in ReefAI's history, and all of them are deleted with your account. If you report an answer of ReefAI as offensive or harmful, the text of that answer and the name of the model are saved with your account, so that the person who runs Notereef can read the report.

ReefAI on your computer can also search the web and read its pages when it needs to (something current, or not in your things): the words it searches for go to Tavily, where you gave its key, or else to DuckDuckGo (see above), and the pages it reads are fetched by the app from your computer, so their sites see your IP address, as when you open them in a browser. Nothing of it is kept but your conversation, with your account (see above).

Instead, ReefAI can run on a model of Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (the models of ChatGPT), under an API key of your own, which is the only way in the phone app and the browser version. There, what you write to ReefAI, the pictures you send it and what it reads to answer you goes straight from your device to the company whose key you gave, under that key, and not through Notereef's servers. That company handles it under the terms of your key: with a free key from Google, Google may use it to improve its products and its reviewers may read it.

ReefAI Cowork, in the Windows app, is ReefAI on your computer that you speak to: once you switch on listening for “Hey Reef” in ReefAI's settings, the app uses your microphone for as long as it runs, also with its window closed (it then waits in the tray, and can start with Windows if you let it). What it hears is written out on your computer by whisper.cpp, which you download in the same place (from GitHub and Hugging Face, see above); only what you say after “Hey Reef” is handed to ReefAI on your computer, which does it there (starting programs and games, looking music up in Spotify, the volume, pages, folders and windows, and clicking and typing in the window in front). To do that it looks at the screen when it needs to: what the window in front shows, and, if you download the part of the model that sees, a picture of the screen, which it looks at on your computer and forgets. If you put the assistant on the screen (a small orb at its edge), everything you say while its panel is open and its microphone on is written out on your computer and handed to ReefAI on your computer, with no “Hey Reef”; parts of the screen you mark for it are read there the same way, what they show and a picture of them. No sound, no text and no picture of it is kept or goes to Notereef or to anyone else. Switched off, the microphone is not used.

These companies are based in the USA. Your content is stored in the European Union; where one of them could reach data from outside the European Economic Area, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long

  • Until you delete it. Deleting a page, an entry or anything else removes it at once.
  • The pictures and files of a deleted page or a deleted chat message, and files deleted from Drive, are removed within a few days.
  • Deleting your account removes, at once, the account and everything it owns: your pages, calendar, tasks, finances, whiteboards and profile picture, and the workspaces, whiteboards, expense groups and organizations you made, for everyone you shared them with.
  • What you added to a workspace or an organization that belongs to someone else stays there as theirs and is no longer connected to you. In someone else's expense group the name you were listed under stays.
  • Technical logs, and any backups the hosting companies hold, expire on their schedule, typically within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data and for a copy of it, have it corrected or deleted, have its use restricted, and object to its use. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: change or delete anything, export your pages (a workspace, then Export), and delete your account (Settings, Account, Delete account, or the account deletion page). For anything else write to barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com; you get an answer within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland it is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

Giving your data is voluntary, but there is no account without an email address and a password, and the app does not work without an account.

Security

The app talks to its servers over encrypted connections only. Passwords are stored as hashes. Rules in the database let only you and the people you share with read your things. The content is not end-to-end encrypted, so the companies that run the servers could technically reach it.

Children

Notereef is not meant for anyone under 16.

ReefAI en tu ordenador también puede, cuando lo necesita (algo actual o que no está en tus cosas), buscar en la web y leer páginas: las palabras que busca van a Tavily, si diste su clave, o si no a DuckDuckGo (ver arriba), y las páginas que lee las descarga la aplicación desde tu ordenador, así que sus sitios ven tu dirección IP, como cuando las abres en un navegador. No se guarda nada de ello salvo tu conversación, con tu cuenta (ver arriba).

ReefAI también puede funcionar con un modelo de Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) u OpenAI (los modelos de ChatGPT), con una clave de API propia; en la aplicación para el teléfono y la versión para el navegador, solo así. Allí, lo que escribes a ReefAI, las imágenes que le envías y lo que lee para responderte va directamente de tu dispositivo a la empresa cuya clave hayas dado, con esa clave, y no pasa por los servidores de Notereef. Cuánto tiempo

  • Hasta que lo elimines. Al eliminar una página, un registro o cualquier otra cosa, desaparece al instante.
  • Las imágenes y los archivos de una página eliminada o de un mensaje de chat eliminado, y los archivos eliminados de Drive, se borran en pocos días.
  • Eliminar tu cuenta borra al instante la cuenta y todo lo que le pertenece: tus páginas, calendario, tareas, finanzas, pizarras y foto de perfil, y los espacios, pizarras, grupos de gastos y organizaciones que creaste, para todas las personas con las que los compartiste.

  • Los registros técnicos y las copias de seguridad que puedan tener las empresas de alojamiento caducan según sus plazos, normalmente en 30 días.

Tus derechos

Según el RGPD puedes pedir acceso a tus datos y una copia de ellos, hacer que se rectifiquen o se supriman, que se limite su uso, y oponerte a su uso. La mayor parte puedes hacerla tú en la aplicación: cambiar o eliminar cualquier cosa, exportar tus páginas (un espacio, luego Exportar) y eliminar tu cuenta (Ajustes, Cuenta, Eliminar cuenta, o la página de eliminación de cuenta). Para cualquier otra cosa escribe a barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com; recibirás respuesta en el plazo de un mes.

También puedes reclamar ante una autoridad de protección de datos. En Polonia es el Presidente de la Oficina de Protección de Datos Personales (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

Dar tus datos es voluntario, pero no hay cuenta sin una dirección de correo electrónico y una contraseña, y la aplicación no funciona sin cuenta.

Seguridad

La aplicación habla con sus servidores solo por conexiones cifradas. Las contraseñas se guardan como hashes.
