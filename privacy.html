Privacy Policy

Notereef is an app for notes, plans and personal finances, for Windows and Android. This page says what the app keeps about you, where, why, and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Patryk Barszczewski, the developer of Notereef. Questions and requests: barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com.

What Notereef keeps

What Details
Your account Your email address; your password, kept only as a hash that nobody can read back; the name you gave; your language, currency and first day of the week; how you arranged Home; a profile picture, if you add one.
What you put in the app Workspaces and pages with their text; calendar events; tasks; finance entries (income and expenses, stocks and other assets, savings goals); shared expenses (groups, the names of the people in them, amounts); whiteboards and what is drawn on them; organizations.
Sharing Who you share a workspace, a whiteboard, an expense group or an organization with, their role, and invitations waiting for an answer.
Activity How long the app was open on each day (a number of seconds per day), for the activity chart on Home. Not what you did in it.
Technical logs The servers the app talks to record the IP address, the time and the kind of each request for a short time, as every web server does.

Notereef has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not read your location, your contacts or other apps. It does not connect to your bank or broker: the finance entries are the ones you type in or import yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Screenshots you import (a bank's, a calendar's, a broker's) are read on your device. The pictures are not sent anywhere; only the entries you confirm are saved to your account.
  • Reminders on Android are scheduled on the phone itself, from what the app has loaded. Nothing is sent to the phone from a server.
  • The app keeps you signed in and remembers a few choices (the last language, reminder settings) in its own storage on the device.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To give you the app: to keep your things, show them on your devices and share them with the people you choose. This is the performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working (the technical logs). This is a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your data is not sold and not used for advertising or profiling, and no decisions about you are made automatically.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • The people you share with. Everyone in a workspace, a whiteboard, an expense group or an organization sees what is in it, and of you: your name, your profile picture and your email address. Someone you invite sees your name on the invitation.
  • A profile picture is kept at a web address that is long, random and listed nowhere, but not protected by a password.
  • The companies that run the servers for Notereef, and only to do that:
Company What it does Where
Supabase, Inc. The database, signing in, and the storage of profile pictures. EU (Ireland)
Render Services, Inc. The server that lets several people edit a page at once. The text of a page passes through it on its way to the database. EU (Frankfurt, Germany)
GitHub, Inc. Hosts this website and the updates of the Windows app. It sees your IP address when you open this site or when the app checks for an update. USA
jsDelivr The first time you import a screenshot, the app downloads from it the files it needs to read text. It sees your IP address, never the screenshot. Worldwide

Prices of stocks and funds in Finance come from Yahoo Finance through Notereef's own server: only the symbol of the stock or fund is sent, nothing about you. If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download under its own privacy policy.

These companies are based in the USA. Your content is stored in the European Union; where one of them could reach data from outside the European Economic Area, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long

  • Until you delete it. Deleting a page, an entry or anything else removes it at once.
  • Deleting your account removes, at once, the account and everything it owns: your pages, calendar, tasks, finances, whiteboards and profile picture, and the workspaces, whiteboards, expense groups and organizations you made, for everyone you shared them with.
  • What you added to a workspace or an organization that belongs to someone else stays there as theirs and is no longer connected to you. In someone else's expense group the name you were listed under stays.
  • Technical logs, and any backups the hosting companies hold, expire on their schedule, typically within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data and for a copy of it, have it corrected or deleted, have its use restricted, and object to its use. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: change or delete anything, export your pages (a workspace, then Export), and delete your account (Settings, Account, Delete account, or the account deletion page). For anything else write to barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com; you get an answer within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland it is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

Giving your data is voluntary, but there is no account without an email address and a password, and the app does not work without an account.

Security

The app talks to its servers over encrypted connections only. Passwords are stored as hashes. Rules in the database let only you and the people you share with read your things. The content is not end-to-end encrypted, so the companies that run the servers could technically reach it.

Children

Notereef is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

  • Lo que añadiste a un espacio o a una organización que pertenece a otra persona se queda allí como suyo y deja de estar vinculado a ti. En el grupo de gastos de otra persona se queda el nombre con el que figurabas.
  • Los registros técnicos y las copias de seguridad que puedan tener las empresas de alojamiento caducan según sus plazos, normalmente en 30 días.

Tus derechos

Según el RGPD puedes pedir acceso a tus datos y una copia de ellos, hacer que se rectifiquen o se supriman, que se limite su uso, y oponerte a su uso. La mayor parte puedes hacerla tú en la aplicación: cambiar o eliminar cualquier cosa, exportar tus páginas (un espacio, luego Exportar) y eliminar tu cuenta (Ajustes, Cuenta, Eliminar cuenta, o la página de eliminación de cuenta). Para cualquier otra cosa escribe a barszczewski.patryk01@gmail.com; recibirás respuesta en el plazo de un mes.

También puedes reclamar ante una autoridad de protección de datos. En Polonia es el Presidente de la Oficina de Protección de Datos Personales (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

Dar tus datos es voluntario, pero no hay cuenta sin una dirección de correo electrónico y una contraseña, y la aplicación no funciona sin cuenta.

Seguridad

La aplicación habla con sus servidores solo por conexiones cifradas. Las contraseñas se guardan como hashes. Las reglas de la base de datos permiten que solo tú y las personas con las que compartes lean tus cosas. El contenido no está cifrado de extremo a extremo, por lo que las empresas que gestionan los servidores podrían técnicamente acceder a él.
